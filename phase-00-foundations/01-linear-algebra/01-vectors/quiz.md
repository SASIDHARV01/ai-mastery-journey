# Vectors quiz

Use x positive east and y positive north for movement questions. Show your reasoning. No answer key is provided before your attempt.

1. You move 2 steps west and 5 steps north. Write the vector and explain what each component means. Would swapping the components represent the same movement?
2. Let a = (2, -1) and b = (-5, 4). Find a + b and -2a. Explain what the negative multiplier does to the direction and what it does to the length.
3. Find the length of v = (-4, 3), then its unit vector. Show the calculations. Why could you not construct a unit vector from (0, 0) using the same rule?
4. Calculate the dot product of (2, 1) and (1, -2). Is your result a scalar or a vector? What does it tell you about these two nonzero movement vectors?
5. A house is represented by (90, 3, 2), ordered as [area in square metres, bedrooms, bathrooms]. What is its dimension? What would (3, 90, 2) mean under the same ordering, and why is the order important? Must these components represent compass directions?
